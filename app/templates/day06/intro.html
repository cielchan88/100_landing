Day 06 · The Shape of a Scam

The math behind three
income structures.

Ponzi schemes, pyramid schemes, and multi-level marketing share more mathematics than the law suggests — and less than the headlines imply. This tool simulates them, side by side.

Before you start

Many people become involved in these structures for understandable reasons — economic pressure, trust in a friend or family member, or the genuine hope of a better life. This tool is not about judgment. It is about making the underlying mathematics visible, so anyone facing a choice can see it clearly before deciding.

Three structures

Ponzi Scheme

Investments paid back from new investors' capital, not real revenue.

  • Promises consistent, sustainable returns
  • No real product or service generating cash
  • Collapses when recruitment slows
Pyramid Scheme

Income comes from recruiting new members, not from selling products or services.

  • Entry fee paid by each new joiner
  • Recruiter earns a cut of each recruit's fee
  • Population mathematically runs out
Multi-Level Marketing

Legal hybrid: real products sold to consumers plus commission from recruited downline.

  • Wholesale buy-in, retail markup on sales
  • Monthly inventory commitments
  • Commission on downline product sales
