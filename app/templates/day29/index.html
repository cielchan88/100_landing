Day 29 · Pluck

Strings, synthesized from physics.

Karplus-Strong synthesis — a burst of noise becomes a plucked string. No samples, no files. Strum to play.

Tap to play
Browsers need a gesture to start audio.
Karplus-Strong (1983): fill a short delay line with noise, then on each pass average two adjacent samples and feed them back at a tiny loss. The noise quickly collapses into a pitched, decaying tone. The delay length sets the pitch (sample rate ÷ frequency). The averaging strength is brightness. The feedback loss is decay. Default tuning is pentatonic so a random strum always sounds musical.
