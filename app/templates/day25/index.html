Day 25 · Permadeath

Descend. Build your deck.
Die. Again.

A small roguelike deckbuilder. One floor, real card combat with telegraphed enemy intents, a handful of relics, permadeath. Win the boss to clear the run.

You start with a humble deck and a single life. Pick your way through the dungeon. Each fight is a small puzzle — read the enemy's intent, spend your energy, play your hand. Win and choose a card to add. Die and start fresh.

How to play

Map: click an adjacent room to move. Combat rooms (⚔) trigger fights, treasure (💎) gives a relic, rest (☾) heals.

Combat: each turn you draw cards. Click a card to play it (some cards target an enemy — click the enemy after). Spend energy (top-left). Block (🛡) resets at the start of every turn. Enemies show what they'll do next: ⚔ = attack, 🛡 = block, ✦ = buff/debuff.

Status: Vulnerable = takes +50% damage. Weak = deals -25% damage. Strength = +damage on each attack.
