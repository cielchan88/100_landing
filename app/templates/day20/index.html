Day 20 · Forty

When do you turn forty
in Hijri years?

A lunar year is eleven days shorter than a solar one. So the fortieth Hijri year arrives more than a year before your fortieth birthday. Enter your birthdate to find the date.

The age of forty is a milestone across many traditions. In the Qur'an, Surah Al-Ahqaf (46:15) describes it as the age of full maturity — the moment a person is called to gratitude and reflection. Measured by the lunar Hijri calendar, that fortieth year comes sooner than the Gregorian count suggests. This tool finds exactly when.
