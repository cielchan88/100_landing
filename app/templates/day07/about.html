About Distraction Inventory.

Most productivity apps track what you did. This one tracks what you didn't do — and what you did instead. Together, those two pieces of information form a more honest picture of your day than any task-completion list.

Capture is intentionally minimal. Two fields, six avoidance categories, eight distraction categories. The constraints exist to keep logging under five seconds. If logging takes longer than that, you'll stop doing it, and the patterns never emerge.

After a week or two of entries, the Insights page shows you where your attention actually goes when you're avoiding something hard. The patterns are usually less surprising than the consistency of the patterns.

Your entries never leave your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data, the entries are gone. There's an Export option on the Insights page if you want a permanent record.
