Day 16 · The 5-Whys Partner

Most problems are not
the real problem.

Five careful "why" questions, then synthesis. A classic root-cause framework, run as a conversation.

The Five Whys is one of the simplest and most powerful problem-solving frameworks ever created — Toyota, 1950s. State a problem. Ask why. Drill deeper. Five times. By the end, you have either reached the root or learned that what you thought was the problem isn't.

Doing it alone fails. Your own reasoning gives up before going deep enough. This is a partner that doesn't.
