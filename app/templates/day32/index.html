How to Play

Goal: reach 3 points. KO an opponent card = 1 point; KO a Legend ★ = 2 points. You also win if the opponent has no card in play.

Deck: 20 cards, max 2 copies per name. No energy cards in the deck.

Setup: each side draws 5 (guaranteed to include a Basic), places 1 Basic as Active, and may fill the Bench (max 3). A coin flip picks who goes first.

Energy Zone: at the start of your turn you receive 1 energy of your deck's type (Rimba → R, Sakti → S). Attach it to any creature of yours, or skip it (it's lost). Exception: the player who goes first gets no energy on their first turn.

Turn: draw 1 → attach energy → freely: play a Basic, evolve, use an Item; max 1 Supporter; retreat once (discard energy equal to retreat cost) → attack (ends your turn) or end turn.

Evolution: a Stage 1 evolves from its named Basic. Not on the turn that creature entered play, and never on a side's first turn. Damage and energy carry over.

Attacks: the cost shows energy pips — typed (R/S) must match; C accepts any energy. Coin flips are 50/50.

Weakness: Rimba and Sakti are weak to each other — +20 damage when hitting weakness (applies to the Active).

KO: when damage ≥ HP, the card and its energy go to the discard; the owner must promote a benched creature. The attacker scores points.

Packs: 2 free per day + 1 for every win vs the AI. 5 cards per pack, at least one Uncommon-or-better.

Nusa TCG uses the modern pocket-TCG system — game mechanics belong to everyone — with a roster, names, and text that are entirely original: the wildlife and legends of the Indonesian archipelago. No server, no tracking; your collection stays in this browser.
