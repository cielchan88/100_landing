    How it works

    Standard Scrabble rules: 100 tiles, 7-tile rack, English ENABLE word list (~168k words). First move must cover the center star. Every word formed must be in the dictionary.

    Claude's AI enumerates every legal move from its rack and plays the highest-scoring one. No lookahead, no heuristics — just greedy points.
