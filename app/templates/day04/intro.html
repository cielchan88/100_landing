Why this exists

Knowledge workers in 2026 increasingly hand off five core reasoning functions to AI assistants: working memory (don't remember — just ask), source evaluation (don't weigh sources — accept the summary), inference (don't deduce — read the conclusion), pattern recognition (don't pattern-match — query the model), estimation (don't reason about quantities — request a number).

Each is a muscle. Muscles atrophy without practice. Reasoning Reps is 5 short daily reps — about five minutes total — designed around the specific skills AI substitutes for. Same puzzles for everyone each UTC day. No timer, deep thinking over speed.

The bet: targeted daily practice on AI-substituted skills beats generic brain-training.
